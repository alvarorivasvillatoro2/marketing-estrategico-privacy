Privacy Policy

App: Marketing Estratégico (com.marketingestrategico.app)
Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Summary

Marketing Estratégico does not collect, transmit or share personal information. Everything you enter in the app is stored only on your own device.

2. Responsible party

Álvaro Rivas Villatoro
Contact: arivasv@gmail.com

3. Information you enter

The app lets you record products, business names, strategy decisions, notes and action plans. This information:

  • is stored locally on the device, within the app's own storage;
  • is not sent to the developer's servers or to third parties;
  • is not accessible to the developer.

4. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for registration, an account, an email address or a password.
  • We do not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We do not include third-party services that collect data.

5. Offline operation

The app works without an internet connection and does not make network communications for its operation.

6. Backups and sharing

  • Download backup: creates a file that you decide where to save or with whom to share using your device's options. The developer does not receive that file.
  • Copy summary / Print: the content is sent only to the destination you choose (another app, a printer, a PDF file).
  • System backup: on Android, the operating system may include the app's data in the automatic backup of your Google account, depending on your device settings. That backup is managed by Google, not by the developer.

7. Control and deletion of your data

You can delete a product from within the app, or erase all data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage from your device settings. Because we keep no copies, there is no data of yours that the developer can delete on your behalf.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including minors.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app later adds features that collect data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date of the last update will be shown.
